About

Hi, I'm savith 👋.

I started my academic journey with a pretty big moment — scoring 174 out of 200 in the Grade 5 Scholarship Examination, which got me into Thurstan College, Colombo 7. That was honestly a turning point for me, and I tried to keep that energy going all the way through school. By the time O/Ls came around, I managed to pull off nine A's, which I was really proud of. A/Ls were a bit more of a grind — I finished with two B's and a C — but it was enough to get me where I wanted to go. Right now I'm doing two degrees at the same time: a BSc in Urban Informatics and Planning at the University of Moratuwa, and a BSc in Electrical Engineering through the Open University of Sri Lanka. Yeah, it's a lot — but I actually enjoy having both worlds going at once. On the urban informatics side, I work a lot with GIS and geospatial tools — things like QGIS, GeoPandas, and PyQGIS — and I've been exploring geospatial machine learning pipelines too, like satellite image classification. On the engineering side, I'm deep into electrical machines — motor rewinding, three-phase induction motors, generator design and stator winding calculations — and I really like applying signal processing and DSP concepts across both fields, which honestly makes the two degrees feel more connected than people might expect. On the programming side, Python is my main language and I've gone pretty deep with it — I use Manim for mathematical animations, Flask for building web apps, and lately I've been getting into vibe coding, just building things fast and seeing where they go. I also build Android apps in Kotlin, and I've been getting into Rust as well. Programming in general is something I genuinely love, not just something I do for uni — it's kind of just how I think and solve problems at this point.
